# Outreach to the AI Agent Protocol CG Chairs

A short, respectful message to the **chairs** of the AI Agent Protocol Community Group.
Its purpose is narrow and deliberate: introduce ATP's progress and **ask them how often
they want to hear from us** — not to propose joint meetings or joint deliverables.

Per W3C guidance: provide updates *to the chairs* and let them decide how to share with
their group. Do not assume a need for joint materials or regular joint meetings; invite
cross-repo issues instead. Raise the idea of joint work only if a clear need emerges.

**Send to:** AI Agent Protocol CG chairs (e.g. gaowei chang and co-chairs) directly —
email or their Slack, not a broadcast to their whole group.

---

## Subject

`ATP CG progress + how often would you like updates?`

## Body

Hi [chair names],

I'm writing as chair of the newly active **Agent Trust Protocol (ATP) Community Group**.
As you know, ATP's charter is to produce open specifications that fill the **Security and
Privacy modules** your group has marked as pending community contribution, so I want to
keep you in the loop without adding overhead to your group.

**Where we are:** we have four draft specifications ready for review and are running a
short asynchronous review with our members before our first call:

- did:atp — a quantum-safe DID method (hybrid Ed25519 + ML-DSA-65)
- Agent trust scoring expressed as W3C Verifiable Credentials
- Privacy-first interaction (pairwise DIDs, selective disclosure)
- Conformance & interoperability (vendor-neutral test vectors)

They're rendered here if you or your members would like to look:
https://agent-trust-protocol.github.io/atp-core/specs/

**My one question:** how often would you like to hear from us, and in what form? I don't
want to over-communicate. A few lightweight options I had in mind:

- A brief written progress update to you (the chairs) on a cadence you choose — quarterly
  is my default guess, but you know your group's rhythm.
- Your members are welcome to raise issues directly on our repo
  (https://github.com/agent-trust-protocol/atp-core/issues), and we're happy to do the
  same on yours — a low-overhead way to stay coordinated without joint meetings.

I'd rather not set up recurring joint meetings unless and until there's a concrete need;
the cross-repo issue channel plus periodic written updates should keep us aligned. But
I'm guided by your preference — if there's a moment where a joint session would help (for
example, when we have an adoption candidate that touches ANP directly), I'll propose it
then.

Thank you for the work your group has done defining the protocol; we're aiming to make
the Security and Privacy pieces something your members can pick up and use.

Best regards,
Larry Lewis
Chair, Agent Trust Protocol Community Group
Sovr Labs

## Editor's notes (do not send)

- **Tone:** deferential and low-overhead. The ask is *their* preferred cadence — let them
  set it. Resist proposing joint deliverables here.
- **One question, not five:** the previous version of these questions (cadence, joint
  meetings, joint materials) collapses into a single "how would you like to stay in
  touch?" Keep it that way.
- **Default cadence:** quarterly written updates is a safe opening offer; adjust to
  whatever they say. Their group meets ~biweekly, so they may want a standing 5-minute
  slot occasionally — be open to it but don't request it.
- **Send privately to chairs**, not to their full mailing list/Slack channel.
